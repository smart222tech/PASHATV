Google TV & Android TV setup, made easier.

Straightforward step-by-step help for getting your streaming device ready, choosing a compatible player, importing a playlist you are authorized to use, and troubleshooting common EPG or playback issues.

1. Prepare your device

Check Wi-Fi, system updates, available storage, and compatibility before installing apps.

2. Choose a player

Use a reputable app compatible with your device and follow its official installation instructions.

3. Add your playlist

Use playlist details supplied by a provider or source you are authorized to access. Keep login details private.

4. Fix common issues

Restart the app and device, check the internet connection, and refresh playlist or EPG data when supported.

Important: This guide covers device setup and troubleshooting. Only use playlists and streams you are legally entitled to access. App availability and menu names can vary by device, country, and software version.
