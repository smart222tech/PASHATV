# Free SEO and indexing checklist — PASHA Tech Guide

## Already in the repository
- `robots.txt` allows crawling and points to `sitemap.xml`.
- `sitemap.xml` lists the English, German and Russian landing pages.
- Landing pages use canonical URLs, meta descriptions and `hreflang` annotations.
- GitHub Pages deployment workflow is configured.

## One-time setup (account owner must do this)
1. Open Google Search Console: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Add a **URL prefix** property: `https://smart222tech.github.io/PASHATV/`.
3. Complete the verification method offered by Google. The required verification must be performed from the account that controls the site; do not share passwords or tokens in chat.
4. Open **Sitemaps** and submit: `sitemap.xml`.
5. Open **URL inspection** and test the home page plus `/de/` and `/ru/`. If available, select **Request indexing** for each.
6. Check the Pages/Indexing report later for crawl errors and indexing status.

## Free secondary search engine
1. Open Bing Webmaster Tools: https://www.bing.com/webmasters/
2. Add the public site and follow the offered verification flow.
3. Submit `https://smart222tech.github.io/PASHATV/sitemap.xml` in the sitemap section.

## Free organic promotion routine
- Publish helpful, original setup and troubleshooting content; link to the most relevant page, not always the home page.
- Post short tutorials on YouTube and Shorts; use the relevant page URL in the description where allowed.
- Create original vertical checklist graphics for Pinterest and share short native videos on Instagram/Facebook.
- Answer relevant questions manually in forums and communities only where links are permitted. Do not spam, scrape members, or automate unsolicited messages.
- Review Search Console queries and impressions weekly, then improve pages around questions real visitors search for.

## Important
Submitting a sitemap or requesting indexing does **not** guarantee indexing or ranking. New sites may take days or weeks to be crawled. Keep pages public, useful, accessible, and focused on lawful device setup and services users are authorized to access.
